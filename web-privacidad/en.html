Your dates stay on your phone

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Memora does not collect, send or sell your data. Everything you enter is stored on your phone. The app has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics tools.

What the app stores

On your phone, and only there, Memora stores what you enter:

  • Names, dates and the type of each date.
  • Phone numbers, likes, notes and gift ideas, if you add them.
  • Photos and texts of your memories, if you add them.
  • The notes you write in the Notes section.
  • Your preferences: theme, language, reminder time, tags and greeting messages.

If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted.

Permissions and what they are for

  • NotificationsTo remind you of your dates. Reminders are created on the phone.
  • Alarms & remindersIf you allow it, so reminders arrive right on time and Android doesn't delay them.
  • Contacts, read onlyOnly if you choose "Import from contacts". Memora reads names, phone numbers and birthday and anniversary dates so you can choose which ones to add. It does not change your contacts or send them anywhere.
  • PhotosOnly when you choose a photo for a memory, using Android's photo picker. The app only accesses the photo you choose.
  • Fingerprint or PINIf you turn on the lock. Android does the check; Memora has no access to your fingerprint.

Backups

Manual backup. When you tap "Export backup", the app creates a file with your data and opens Android's Share menu. You choose where to save it, for example Google Drive. That file stays under your control.

Android backup. If it is turned on on your phone, Android may include Memora's data in your Google account backup, like with any other app. That is handled by Google according to your settings, not by Memora.

Greetings, cards and widget

When you tap "Say hi" (WhatsApp, SMS or call) or share a card, Memora opens the app you choose with the message or image ready. You send it yourself from that app; Memora never sends anything on its own.

The home screen widget shows your upcoming dates by reading the data stored on the phone.

Children

Memora is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this same page with the updated date.

For questions about this policy, write to us through Memora's contact section on Google Play.
